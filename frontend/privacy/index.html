Privacy Policy for SmartBilling

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartBilling. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Data We Collect

When you use Google Sign-In to access SmartBilling, we collect the following information:

  • Your primary email address
  • Your basic profile information (such as your name)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and allow you to log in to the SmartBilling application securely.
  • To associate your billing and inventory data with your unique user profile.
  • To personalize your experience by displaying your name on the dashboard.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Your data is strictly used for the core functionality of the SmartBilling application.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy policy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you may revoke access via your Google Account settings, or contact us directly to have your data permanently erased from our database.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the project's GitHub repository or email.
